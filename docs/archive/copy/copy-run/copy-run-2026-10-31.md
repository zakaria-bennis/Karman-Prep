# Copy run, 2026-10-31

The website copy in `.impeccable/mocks/decision/film/copy.js`, rewritten for the
October 30 plan ladder with the conversion-copywriter skill. This file replaces
the 2026-10-25 run (`copy-deck.html` and `copy.json`, deleted: the cohort has no
1:1 reviews any more, Private is $1,495 and $2,495, scores are one number, and
the monthly plans exist).

The method is Harry Dry's (marketingexamples.com, from his _How I Write_
interview with David Perel): no writing until there are facts, every line built
on a real problem the buyer has, the climb from abstract to concrete, and three
rules every load-bearing line has to clear: can you see it, can it be proved
wrong, and could nobody else say it.

What ships is the chosen line in `copy.js`. Everything else here is the
reasoning, so the founder can pick a different line without re-doing the work.

## 1. Intake

- **Format.** Landing page and its inner pages. The click is already paid for;
  the job is keeping the promise of the ad or the search result.
- **What it is, literally.** An SAT practice platform (Free, Platform, Plus),
  two live programs with a guarantee (the SAT Program, Private 1:1), and a
  school license. Every student gets Your Daily 10 (from Platform), a Karman
  tutor in every live class, a calendar with every due date, full-length tests
  with a Karman predicted score, and a weekly update for parents.
- **Point A, the parent.** Has seen "+200 points guaranteed" ads and expects
  the rules to be on a terms page. Has paid for prep before and watched the
  homework stop after week two. Cannot see what is happening until the score
  arrives. Is comparing a free app, a big-brand course and a local tutor by the
  hour, and cannot tell from any of their sites what the student actually gets.
- **Point B.** The parent can tell exactly what the student gets, screen by
  screen, with the guarantee's every rule on the page, and takes the first step:
  the free practice test on December 12.
- **The offer.** Only from `src/lib/marketing/offer.ts` and
  `docs/business/plan-ladder-2026-10-30.md`. Section 4 lists every fact used.
- **Reviews.** None. There are no paying users yet. No quote was written; the
  testimonials section stays empty until the founder adds real ones (design rule
  12). The only results on the site are the labelled 1:1 track record.

## 2. The enemy

Type: approach. **SAT prep that prints a big promise, keeps the rules on a terms
page, and stops watching once the family has paid.** Karman prints the whole
guarantee on the front page, shows the product screen by screen, and tells the
parent every week whether the practice happened.

That enemy shapes the home page: the opening (the fine print on the front
page), the guarantee block (every rule in view), and the parent update (the
work, every week). The other sections are the product itself, because a page
with no real product demo is on the founder's never list (rule 18).

## 5. The interrogation: abstract to concrete

The ladders behind the lines that changed most.

- "Transparent guarantee" → the rules are on the page → which rules? four, all
  numbers → where? the home page, in type this size → **The fine print, on the
  front page.**
- "Spaced review keeps them practising" → the question comes back → when? 2, 7
  and 21 days → **Every miss comes back three times.**
- "Real tutors, not videos" (banned pattern) → who teaches? a Karman tutor, every
  live class and the weekly session → and you can meet one: the free results
  call → **Karman tutors teach every live class.**
- "Parents stay informed" → what do they see? practice time, which skills moved,
  scores → how often? every week → **Every week, you see the practice time.**
- "Small classes" → how small? five paid seats → and? one scholarship seat in
  every cohort → **Five paid seats, one scholarship seat.**
- "Planned, not open-ended" (dropped positioning) → what happens in a session?
  the latest test, then an hour on the misses → **An hour on every mistake, one
  to one.**
- "Proven results" → whose? 1:1 students → how much? 285 points, 1235 to 1520 →
  with the label → **From 1235 to 1520, on average.**
- "Our story" → a tutor → how many students? 1,200+ → **Built by a tutor with
  1,200+ students.**

## 9. Cut, and why

- "The program ends the day your student sits the SAT", "planned back from the
  test", "ends on your student's test date", "SAT prep should have a finish
  line": the positioning the founder dropped on October 26.
- "Sell open-ended hours": false now that Platform and Plus are monthly plans.
- "Every session starts with a test, not a worksheet" and "Our prediction, not
  an official score": the "not X, Y" pattern.
- "Spaced review is one of the best-supported methods in learning research"
  and "what research keeps backing": research claims with no source on the page.
- "Small enough that every student answers out loud", "Built for an 11th
  grader", "the eight SAT domains", "half the length of the SAT", "no materials
  fee, no platform fee": not in the offer.
- "Every cohort's results published, dropouts included": a promise nobody owns
  yet.
- "Texts from Karman 1:1 students, shared with permission": no real screens
  yet; the section is empty until they exist.
