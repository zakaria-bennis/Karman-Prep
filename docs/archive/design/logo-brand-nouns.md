# Karman logo: brand nouns

Method from Allan Peters (_Logos That Last_, and his interviews):

1. List about 70 brand nouns. Abstract ideas become concrete things you can
   draw (trust becomes a lighthouse, the return of a missed question becomes a
   boomerang).
2. The founder crosses out every noun that feels wrong, leaving 10 to 15.
3. Pair the survivors with the letter K and with each other, using five tools:
   **negative space, overlapping geometry, visual rhythm, an unexpected twist on
   an expected visual, and visual flow.**
4. Sketch many; vectorise the promising ones; only then ask whether a
   negative-space solution exists.
5. Present about 15 marks, in black on white and white on black only (no
   colour), each labelled with its nouns. Pick a gut-reaction top three.
6. Build the top three into full systems: horizontal and stacked lockups, an
   icon and a badge.

**Fixed core for Karman:** the letter K and the Kármán line, the boundary
100 km up where the sky ends and space begins. In plain terms, the line you
rise past. Every mark combines the K, the line and at most one more noun.
