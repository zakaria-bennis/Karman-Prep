# Karman calculator: UX, keypad, accessibility and theme

This part of the [Karman calculator design](../2026-10-16-calculator-design.md)
defines what a student sees, touches and hears in the Karman calculator: the calculator
window and its layouts, expression rows, typing behaviour and MathQuill configuration, the desktop
and mobile keypads and the functions panel, settings, keyboard-first operation, the Scientific
calculator, animation, screen-reader and audio support, and how every colour is derived from the
calculator's 20 themes (TH-5) so that the approved contrast targets hold. Each checklist line is a testable
acceptance item with its phase and test layer. Math meaning (parsing, evaluation, number display,
graphing, points of interest) lives in `a-math-semantics.md`; function definitions and data in
`b-functions-and-data.md`; Transfer to Calculator in `c-transfer.md`; architecture, the MathField
adapter and the public API in `d-architecture-integration.md`.

IDs defined here:

- Layout and window: L-1 to L-18
- Rows: R-1 to R-11
- Typing: T-1 to T-13
- Keypad: K-1 to K-13
- Settings: S-1 to S-9
- Keyboard: KB-1 to KB-14
- Scientific mode: SC-1 to SC-15
- Animation: AN-1 to AN-14
- Screen readers and audio: A11Y-1 to A11Y-15
- Theme and contrast: TH-1 to TH-18

## Conventions

- Line format: `**ID** statement. (Phase N; verified by <layer> test)`. Layers: unit, component
  (Vitest + RTL), browser (Playwright, `playwright.calculator.config.ts` projects), visual,
  contrast, perf.
- "Confirm by REA behaviour capture in Phase N" marks a default written from general mathematical
  and UI knowledge. The lead confirms or adjusts it with owner-authorised REA capture (timing and
  screenshots only) and records the result in that phase's plan. No Desmos code, scripts, DOM,
  assets, icons, fonts, logo or exact visual design is copied.
- "Karman addition" marks behaviour the owner asked for that is not part of test-day parity.
- "Owner-confirmed" marks a decision or recommended default the owner confirmed on 2026-10-16;
  the overview's owner-interpretations list records each one.

## 1. Layouts and the calculator window

Breakpoint terms used below: **wide** means viewport width at least 1024 px and viewport height
greater than 500 px, whatever the input device (touch tablets included, with coarse-pointer key
sizes); **narrow** means anything else. Docking and the floating window apply only on wide
viewports, and narrow viewports open full screen (owner-confirmed).

- **L-1** Desktop layout (calculator container at least 450 px wide, at any height; RS-1): expression list on the left at `clamp(320px, 160px + 20%, 500px)`, or the width the student dragged its resize divider to (320 px to the container width minus 200 px, kept across later resizes; either width snaps to the one that leaves the graph square when within 10 px of it, as the reference's does; the divider is also a focusable separator moved with the arrow keys, Home and End), graph on the right at the full height; the open keypad lies across the bottom over both, so the graph never resizes for it; list toolbar in this order: Add item, Undo, Redo, Edit list, Collapse list; graph controls 5 px from the top right as 37 px pills: Settings (wrench), the Zoom in / Zoom out group, Home only while the view differs from the default, and under Home the Karman Zoom to fit button (L-17). (amended 2026-10-19 for the reference look, rea-targets visual MASTER.md) (Phase 1; verified by browser test)
- **L-2** Stacked layout (calculator container narrower than 450 px): graph on top at 42% of the height, then a toolbar with Add item, Keypad toggle (fine pointers), Undo, Redo, Edit list, Collapse list, then the expression list, then the keypad when open, which takes its height from the list; graph controls at the top right of the graph; on a coarse pointer with a calculator under 450 by 495 px the zoom group is hidden. (amended 2026-10-19 for the reference look, rea-targets visual MASTER.md) (Phase 1; verified by browser test)
- **L-3** Rows are numbered from 1 in a 38 px left gutter; the focused (or active) row fills its whole gutter cell in the focused-row colour, with the row number in its label colour, and takes 2 px accent borders top and bottom; a row focused from the keyboard also shows a 2 px focus-ring outline drawn 2 px inside its edge (outline-offset -2px), so the mouse look matches the reference while keyboard focus stays visible (colours from TH-3; appendix F). (amended 2026-10-19 for the reference look, rea-targets visual MASTER.md) (Phase 1, amended in the parity work; verified by visual test)
- **L-4** Opening outside a transfer: the quiz toolbar Calculator button and the mobile side button open the docked calculator (L-7) on wide viewports and full screen (L-11) on narrow viewports; the first open on a wide viewport docks, and later opens, after a close, a question change or a reload, come back in the mode (floating, docked or maximized), place and size the student last left (AR-25; owner decision, 2026-10-21; appendix F: the reference sits beside the question); docked and maximized windows open without the AN-10 entry animation; both buttons stay available only in Math questions, as today. (Phase 1, amended in the parity work; verified by browser test)
- **L-5** The calculator uses `CALCULATOR_DOCK_MIN_WIDTH = 1024` and `FULL_SCREEN_QUERY` (width 1023 px or less, or height 500 px or less) from `src/components/calculator/breakpoints.ts` (AR-19), so docking and the floating window need a viewport at least 1024 px wide and more than 500 px tall, and every other viewport opens full screen. (Phase 1; verified by browser test)
- **L-6** The floating window offers: drag by the title bar, resize from a corner handle (minimum 360 x 280 px), Maximize and Restore, Minimize (hides the window and keeps its work), default size `min(880px, 100vw - 32px)` by `min(600px, 100dvh - 80px)` at (16, 64), and position clamped inside the viewport on every resize; in the quiz the window sits at z-index 72 in every mode, below the scratchpad at 74. (Phase 1; verified by browser test)
- **L-7** On a wide viewport a transfer docks the calculator as a left column of the quiz workspace, default width `clamp(400px, 45vw, 720px)`, resizable with a separator (role `separator`, pointer drag, Left/Right arrow keys in 16 px steps, Home and End) between 400 px and `min(900px, 100vw - 480px)` (AR-23), with the question column forced to a single column while docked. (Phase 1; verified by browser test)
- **L-8** The calculator picks L-1 or L-2 from its own container width (ResizeObserver on the host), not from the viewport: L-1 when the container is at least 450 px wide, at any height, L-2 below that, as the reference calculator does. (amended 2026-10-19: owner decision replaces the 560 px / 1.2-aspect rule) (Phase 1; verified by browser test)
- **L-9** A transfer while the floating window is open converts it to the dock and keeps all rows; dock state lives in the window store `calculator-window-store.ts`, kept in localStorage (AR-25), so docking persists across Previous, Next and a reload while the calculator contents still reset per question; the same store carries the student's settings from question to question for the page session (S-9). (Phase 1; verified by browser test)
- **L-10** Closing the dock or floating window restores the full question width in the same frame and returns focus to the opener (the Transfer control or toolbar button); if the opener is gone, focus goes to the toolbar Calculator button. (Phase 1; verified by browser test)
- **L-11** Full screen (narrow viewports, including phone landscape at height 500 px or less): a modal dialog covering the viewport, with the quiz workspace inert and its scroll locked, Tab contained in the calculator, and focus returned to the opener on close. (Phase 1; verified by browser test)
- **L-12** The window title bar (light DOM, outside the test-day UI) holds, in order: the label "Karman calculator", the Graphing/Scientific mode control (SC-1), the Paste button (L-13, not built yet), Keyboard shortcuts (KB-1), the window controls of the current mode from the AR- mode table (floating: Maximize, Minimize; docked: Undock, Maximize, Minimize; maximized: Restore, Minimize; full screen: none), and Close; its colours come from the TH-5 eager chrome sheet (TH-2). (Phase 1; verified by component test)
- **L-13** The Paste button reads the clipboard (plain text, LaTeX, CSV, TSV or a single HTML table), inserts math as a row or a table as an x₁/y₁ table using the paste rules shared with Transfer (`c-transfer.md`), falls back to a labelled "Math to paste" text box with Insert and Cancel when clipboard reading is denied, and rejects image and drop input with a status message. (Phase 1, not built yet: AR-14; to be verified by browser test)
- **L-14** At every supported size from 320 x 568 to 1920 x 1080 the calculator has no horizontal scroll (WCAG 1.4.10), and the focused row is never covered by the keypad, toolbar or a popover (WCAG 2.4.11): the list scrolls the focused row into view with bottom scroll padding equal to the keypad height. (Phase 1; verified by browser test)
- **L-16** Rows draw no border, fill or box around unfocused math, and one 1 px rule in the boundary role separates each row from the next (appendix F). (Phase 3, built in the parity work; verified by browser test)
- **L-17** Zoom to fit (Karman addition; owner decision, 2026-10-21; the reference has no such control): a graph-control button named "Zoom to fit" (same name as its tooltip) sits under Home. It moves the view so every visible graphed item's key points fit with about 10% of the view spare on each side, keeping the current x:y units per pixel so a circle stays round. Key points are the points of interest of every traceable row (zeros, intercepts, extrema, intersections), plotted points, table points (a regression's data), the positions of vertical and horizontal lines, and a curve's whole extent when it is finite (it ends inside the view, as a circle does). It animates like Home (AN-7). It is hidden while nothing is graphed and while the viewport is locked (S-8). (Phase 2; verified by unit and browser test, `fit-view.test.ts`, `tests/calculator/graph-ux.spec.ts`)
- **L-18** One-time curve hint (owner decision, 2026-10-21): the first time a student's own edit graphs a curve (a curve, line, region or table regression; a question's preset graph never counts), a small quiet line under the graph says "Tap a curve to see key points", or "Click a curve to see key points" with a fine pointer. It is a polite status, goes on the student's first press anywhere in the calculator except a keypad key or the math being typed, or after 6 s, and never shows again: localStorage remembers it (`karman-calculator:curve-hint-seen`), and when storage fails it shows at most once per page load. (Phase 2; verified by component test)

## 2. Expression rows

- **R-1** Each row's gutter tab shows its curve colour and line or point style; activating it hides or shows the graph of that row, with `aria-pressed` reflecting visibility. (Phase 1; verified by component test)
- **R-2** A row with an error shows an orange warning marker (a rounded triangle with an exclamation mark, about 22 px wide, in the warning-orange role) in the gutter slot where the colour tab would be, instead of the tab, so an error row offers no hide or show toggle; it does not plot. The plain-language message (wording from `a-math-semantics.md`) is not shown inline: it is a tooltip with 14 px text in the error tooltip tone and its label colour (10.3: grey and white on the classic skin, a lighter tone of the skin's ink on the site skins), at most 240 px wide, directly below the marker with an arrow centred on it, drawn over the rows below and never clipped by the row. The tooltip opens at once on pointer hover and on keyboard focus of the marker, toggles on a tap with a touch or coarse pointer, and closes on pointer leave, blur, Escape (one KB-3 layer) and a press outside; it fades in over 100 ms, with no motion under reduced motion. The row's textbox keeps `aria-invalid="true"` and is described by the same message (A11Y-3), and the marker is a button named "Error" described by it. A table cell or header that cannot evaluate (D-6, D-10) shows the same marker, about 16 px wide, at its left with the same tooltip, and its field is `aria-invalid` and described by the message. (Phase 1; verified by component test)
- **R-3** Each row has a Delete control; Enter creates a new row below and focuses it; Up and Down move between rows when the caret cannot move further up or down inside the math. (Phase 1; verified by browser test)
- **R-4** Rows can be reordered by drag, duplicated, and given a colour (the six slots of TH-10) and a line style (solid, dashed, dotted) or point style (filled, open, cross) from a style picker opened from the gutter tab by long-press, right-click or the Edit list menu. (Phase 2; verified by browser test)
- **R-5** A row that evaluates to a number or list shows its value right-aligned on its own line below the math: a muted "=" (secondary text) and then the value in a small box (raised key background, 1 px field-boundary border, 3 px radius, padding 2 px 8 px), formatted by the number-display rules of `a-math-semantics.md`. A list shows one such box per element after the "=", wrapping onto further lines, the first 30 elements and then a last box holding "…" (NM-38); assistive technology reads "equals 1, 2, 3, …". (Phase 1; verified by component test)

  The decimal/fraction toggle (Phase 2; captured from live Desmos 2026-10-21; verified by unit, component and Playwright tests): an expression row that does not graph and whose value is one real number with a fraction form (NM-7) shows the toggle in its gutter where the colour icon would be: a computed value, a definition, `f(2)`, and a literal such as `0.75` whose value is otherwise unshown. A slider row offers it too, but its Play button takes the gutter, so only the shortcut reaches it. Lists, points, complex values, graphed rows (`y=0.5`, `x=0.5`), relations, tables and regressions offer none, nor does a row that is a bare regression parameter such as `m` after `y_1\sim mx_1+b`, even when it is rational (owner decision, 2026-10-21); degree mode and complex mode keep it for a real value. The toggle is a 29 px circle with a 2 px ink ring and a glyph of two stacked boxes over and under a bar, at opacity 0.5 (0.7 hovered, 0.9 pressed); on, the circle fills with ink and the glyph takes the list colour; on a focused or selected row the ring and glyph turn to the tab label colour at 0.9 and, on, the circle fills with it and the glyph takes the tab's blue. Its name is "Displaying as decimal. Press Enter to display as Fraction" or "Displaying as fraction. Press Enter to display as Decimal"; Enter, Space, a click or a tap switches it, a click leaves focus where it was, and there is no tooltip. On, the value box holds the stacked fraction typeset at 17.48 px with 2 px around it (7/12 makes a 42 × 45.6 box) and is read "equals" and the fraction's speech; a toggled literal or slider shows its value line too, though the API still reports it undisplayed. The choice is per row, one undo step, saved in the state as `displayEvaluationAsFraction: true`, and kept through edits, a whole-number value and an emptied row. Alt+Shift+A (Cmd+Shift+A on a Mac) switches the focused row when that row offers the toggle and announces "Show as Fraction" or "Show as Decimal"; on a focused row that offers no toggle (a regression parameter, a graphed row, a table, a whole number) it does nothing and announces nothing (owner decision, 2026-10-21).

  Click to copy (owner decision, 2026-10-22; Karman's own, Desmos has none; verified by unit, component and Playwright tests): the "=" and the value boxes are one button named "Copy value". A click, a tap, Enter or Space copies the value exactly as shown, without the T-10 thin spaces (`12345` for a box showing 12 345; `7/12` for a row shown as a fraction; a list's shown boxes joined by ", "), through `navigator.clipboard.writeText` with a hidden-textarea `execCommand("copy")` fallback. Once the text is on the clipboard a small Karman-drawn check and "Copied", in the row's small muted caption type (12.8 px), float at the value's top-right corner, over the delete-button column and on the list's own fill, for 1.2 s and then fade over 0.3 s (instantly under reduced motion), and the status region announces "Copied". The note is out of flow, so the value never moves while it shows (the value line ends 5 px from the panel edge, leaving no room beside it); it stays inside the panel on desktop and phone. A failed copy shows and says nothing. A pointer press leaves focus where it was; the fraction toggle beside the value never copies. The textbox's description stays "equals …" (the hidden value text carries the id).

- **R-6** A row whose only problem is undefined names shows the warning marker with "k is not defined." (one name) or "m and b are not defined." (more) in its gutter on the warning-coloured row and, under the math, Desmos's `add slider:` line (owner decision, 2026-10-22; measured on live Desmos 2026-10-22): "add slider:" in 12.8 px muted text, then one 35 × 26 px button per name in order of first appearance, 3 px apart, the first 117.1 px from the row's left edge, then an accent-filled `all`. Name buttons have 3 px corners, a light gradient (#f6f6f6 to #eff2f3) behind a light border and the name in 15.64 px italic math; `all` is 13.6 px upright. One undefined name is a single accent button and no `all`; at most four name buttons show (`y=ax^2+bx+c+d+f_1` shows a, b, c, d, all) and `all` still adds every name. The line makes the row 27 px taller (56.2 to 83.2 px), on desktop and phone alike. Hovered or keyboard-focused, a name button turns flat #eff2f3 with a #bbb edge and `all` goes 10 % toward the ink (rgb(42, 103, 198)), 18 % pressed with no shadow; the keyboard ring is 2 px solid. A button adds `name=1` rows right below the row in one undo step. Enter in such a row (typed, or the keypad's Enter key) with one to four undefined names adds them the same way, in order of first appearance, then an empty row that takes focus, all in one undo step; a lone name (`a`, `x_1`), five or more names, a slider row, blur, Tab and Down add none (SLIDERS-36; observed on live Desmos and confirmed in its shipped code with REA, 2026-10-22). Tab goes from the math to the row's delete cross, then to each button in order, as on Desmos; Enter or Space on a button returns focus to the row's math (SLIDERS-8), a click or tap leaves no focus ring. The line is a group named "add slider:"; buttons are named "Add slider for m" and "Add sliders for all". A slider whose limits use undefined names shows the same line 8 px under its limits editor. (Phase 2; verified by component and browser test)
- **R-7** Sliders show min, max and step fields, the value, and a Play/Pause control; play options are speed (0.25x, 0.5x, 1x, 2x, 4x) and loop mode (back and forth, forward loop, play once), defaulting to 1x and back and forth (confirm by REA behaviour capture in Phase 2). (Phase 2; verified by browser test)
- **R-8** Edit list mode shows, for every row, Delete, Duplicate, Move up, Move down and the style picker, plus a Clear all button at the top of the list (KB-5) and a Done button that leaves the mode. (Phase 2; verified by component test)
- **R-9** Backspace in an empty row deletes it and focuses the end of the previous row; Delete in an empty row does nothing, as on the reference (amended 2026-10-20, break-ui scout delete-spam-10-empty); the last remaining row is never deleted, only emptied. (Phase 1; verified by browser test)
- **R-10** A new question, Clear all and `setBlank()` all leave exactly one empty row, numbered 1; after Clear all that row has focus (KB-5), and after a new question or `setBlank()` it receives focus when the calculator is next opened (KB-4). (Phase 1; verified by component test)
- **R-11** A row whose math uses a trig function (sin, cos, tan, sec, csc, cot, their arc forms or a `^{-1}` inverse; not the hyperbolic functions) shows a small quiet badge "DEG" or "RAD" for the current angle mode, after its value or, with no value line, at the row's bottom right. It is not a control: it takes no focus, is aria-hidden, follows the setting at once, and on a desktop pointer its tooltip reads "Angle mode. Change it in Settings." (owner decision, 2026-10-21) (Phase 2; verified by component and browser test)

## 3. Typing and MathQuill configuration

Each rule is a behaviour of the MathField adapter (`d-architecture-integration.md`). It is
configured through options of the pinned MathQuill commit `1cbc1c4128` where that commit offers
them (for example `autoSubscriptNumerals`, `charsThatBreakOutOfSupSub`, `autoCommands`,
`autoOperatorNames`, `restrictMismatchedBrackets`, `sumStartsWithNEquals`,
`supSubsRequireOperand`, `substituteTextarea`), and in the adapter otherwise.

- **T-1** `^` opens an exponent; Right arrow at the end of the exponent leaves it; `-` typed as the first character of an empty exponent stays inside (`x^{-1}`). (Phase 1; verified by component test)
- **T-2** `/` turns the preceding term into the numerator of a stacked fraction and puts the caret in the denominator; typing `y=x^2+3/4` yields `y=x^{2}+\frac{3}{4}`. (Phase 1; verified by component test)
- **T-3** `alpha`, `beta` and `rho` become α, β and ρ (the only other Greek names Desmos converts while typing; `gamma` stays letters, live Desmos 2026-10-22), `sqrt` becomes a square root, `cbrt` a cube root, `nthroot` an n-th root, `pi` π, `theta` θ, `tau` τ, `phi` φ, `infinity` and `infty` ∞, `sum` Σ with lower and upper limits, `prod` Π, `int` ∫; every non-member name in the PR-12 allowlist module (PR-12's member names are excluded) `src/lib/calculator/core/latex/operator-names.ts` (the `b-functions-and-data.md` function names plus `with`, `for`, `and`, `or`, `of`) is configured as a MathQuill operator name, and the auto-commands listed above come from the same module. (Phase 1; verified by component test)
- **T-4** `<=` and `>=` become ≤ and ≥; `_` opens a subscript; a digit typed directly after a letter becomes a subscript (`x1` gives `x_{1}`), matching the x₁/y₁ table names. A subscript keeps everything typed in it, as on Desmos (live typing and REA reading of its MathQuill configuration, 2026-10-22): brackets, `^`, `_` and `/` build inside it, so `y_{12}=40` gives `y_{\left\{12\right\}}=40`, `x_1^2` gives `x_{1^{2}}`, `y_1{x>0}` gives `y_{1\left\{x>0\right\}}` and `f_1(x)` gives `f_{1\left(x\right)}`; only Right arrow or a T-6 character leaves it. `_` or `^` with nothing before it is ignored (`supSubsRequireOperand`: `x__1` gives `x_{1}`, `^2` gives `2`). A typed backslash is the `\backslash` symbol. `tests/calculator/subscript-typing.spec.ts` holds the observed cases. (Phase 1; amended 2026-10-22; verified by component and browser test)
- **T-5** LaTeX copy and paste round-trips inside and between rows, and each row exposes MathSpeak-style speech text, for example "y equals x squared plus 3 fourths" and "y equals Start Root, x, End Root plus pi". (Phase 1; verified by component test)
- **T-6** `+`, `-`, `=`, `<`, `>` and `*` typed at the end of a non-empty exponent or subscript leave it before inserting the character, so typing `x^2-1` gives `x^{2}-1` and `x_1*2` gives `x_{1}\cdot2`; every other character, `~`, `,` and `:` included, stays inside (`x_1,` gives `x_{1,}`), matching Desmos's `charsThatBreakOutOfSupSub` of `+-=<>*` (REA and live typing, 2026-10-22). (Phase 1; amended 2026-10-22; verified by component and browser test)
- **T-7** `*` displays as a centred dot (`\cdot`), `~` displays as `\sim`, three dots `...` display as an ellipsis inside lists and ranges, and `%` typed from the keyboard or the keypad (Shift on the letters page) writes Desmos's "% of", `\%\operatorname{of}`, as one unit with the caret after it, whatever comes before it (a number, a name, `x+`, a bracket or another "% of"); Backspace deletes it as a unit, so `5% of 40` is typed `5%40` and shows 2, and `[5%a,b` gives `\left[5\%\operatorname{of}a,b\right]` with sliders offered for a and b (amended 2026-10-22 from live Desmos, owner decision). (Phase 1; verified by component and browser test)
- **T-8** Typing `(`, `[`, `{` or `|` inserts a matching pair with the caret inside; typing the closing character steps over it; mismatched brackets are not allowed. (Phase 1; verified by component test)
- **T-9** Typing a function name does not insert parentheses automatically; the keypad and functions-panel buttons insert the name with an empty parenthesis pair and the caret inside (confirm by REA behaviour capture in Phase 1). (Phase 1; verified by component test)
- **T-10** The integer part of a number renders with thin-space groups of three in rows and results (confirm by REA behaviour capture in Phase 1); grouping is rendering only and never appears in the stored LaTeX, the NM- display strings used by the corpus and the API, or speech text. (Phase 1; verified by component test)
- **T-11** The MathField's hidden textarea has `autocapitalize="off"`, `autocorrect="off"`, `spellcheck="false"`, and an `inputmode` set by the adapter: `none` while the on-screen keypad is open, `text` otherwise, so the phone keyboard never appears over the keypad; a touch that is about to focus a row (and so opens the keypad, K-2) sets `none` before the focus, so a phone never starts to raise its own keyboard under the opening keypad (owner report 2026-10-22, a white flash over the rows). (Phase 1; amended 2026-10-22; verified by browser test, `tests/calculator/keypad-flash.spec.ts`)
- **T-12** Pasting plain text into a row normalises calculator notation (`sqrt(x)`, `x^2`, `pi`, `<=`, `x1`) into the same LaTeX that typing it would produce; pasting a table into a row goes through L-13 instead; pasting a selection of rendered question math inserts the math once, taken from the `application/x-tex` annotation in the `text/html` clipboard when present and otherwise from the plain text with KaTeX's repeated MathML text removed, and Unicode operators (² ³ − × · ÷ ≤ ≥ π √) are normalised by PR-4 and TR-7; corpus cases cover a copied inline segment, a copied display segment and a Unicode-only string. (Phase 1; verified by unit test)
- **T-13** A row accepts up to 2,048 characters of LaTeX; a longer paste or insertion is refused with a status message and leaves the row unchanged. (Phase 1; verified by component test)

## 4. Keypad

### 4.1 Desktop tray

The tray observed on the public graphing calculator on 2026-10-16 (capture b1) is three groups of keys:

| Group  | Row 1        | Row 2      | Row 3              | Row 4               |
| ------ | ------------ | ---------- | ------------------ | ------------------- |
| Left   | x, y, a², aᵇ | (, ), <, > | \|a\|, comma, ≤, ≥ | ABC, audio, √, π    |
| Centre | 7, 8, 9, ÷   | 4, 5, 6, × | 1, 2, 3, −         | 0, ., =, +          |
| Right  | Functions    | ←, →       | Backspace          | Enter (accent fill) |
