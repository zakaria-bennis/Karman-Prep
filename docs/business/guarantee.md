# The Karman guarantee (founder, 2026-11-08)

Replaces the 2026-10-25 proposal ("+100 points or a 1450", cohort and Private 20 only).
**Status: the rules are final from the founder (2026-11-08) and reviewed by a lawyer (founder, 2026-11-09). The targets were tiered on 2026-11-09 and still need the lawyer's read.** The numbers live
in `src/lib/marketing/offer.ts` (`GUARANTEE`), and the public page is `/guarantee`.

## Which plans carry it

The 9-week Cohort ($995), Private 10 ($1,495) and Private 20 ($2,495). Free, Practice, Pro, school
licenses and the tutor affiliate plan carry no guarantee: no tutor teaches the student every week.

## The target

Tiered by starting score and by how the student is taught (founder, 2026-11-09, replacing "+100, or +50
from 1400" the same day the rules below were reviewed by a lawyer). **The lawyer has not yet re-read the
targets or the Terms section that states them.**

| Baseline | Private 20 | Cohort and Private 10 |
|---|---|---|
| 1200 or lower | +200 points | +150 points |
| 1210 to 1300 | +150 | +100 |
| 1310 to 1400 | +100 | +70 |
| 1410 to 1450 | +50 | +40 |
| 1460 or higher | No guarantee | No guarantee |

From a baseline of 1460 the plans carry no guarantee, and the family is told before paying. An official
1600 always counts as reaching the target. The baseline is an official SAT or PSAT from the last 6 months,
or the week-1 Bluebook test, proctored live by Karman.

## The Cohort: money back or a free repeat

| Official SAT result | What the family gets |
|---|---|
| The target or more | Nothing back. The promise was kept |
| Half the target or more (for example +75 to +149 from a 1200 baseline; +20 to +39 from 1410 to 1450) | Half the price back ($497.50), or the next cohort free. The family chooses |
| Under half the target | The full $995 back, or the next cohort free. The family chooses |

**Who it covers**
- A student's first Karman program, once per student.
- Paid in full, or every installment paid on time.

**What the student does** (every rule is a number shown on the student's dashboard)
- Attend at least 15 of the 18 live classes, live. A recording doesn't count.
- Take all five full-length practice tests, each in one timed sitting, in the week it's assigned.
- Complete at least 90% of the assigned weekly practice.
- Sit the SAT within 60 days of the last class.

The old rule "complete all five one-on-one reviews" is gone: the cohort has no 1:1s.

## Private 10 and Private 20

Same targets as the cohort.
- The student attends every session. Cancelling with less than 24 hours' notice counts as a missed session.
- Full-length practice tests: two on Private 10, four on Private 20.
- The student sits the SAT within 30 days of the last session.
- If the score falls short on Private 10: half the price back ($747.50) (founder, 2026-11-08).
- If the score falls short on Private 20: up to 6 free extra hours first. If the
  next SAT still falls short, half the price back.

## How to claim

Within 30 days of scores coming out, email the College Board score report to
guarantee@karmanprep.com. One email. No mailed paper reports, no fees taken out of the refund.

**What Karman will not add:** a "good faith" clause, quotas on answering polls, a same-device rule,
fees subtracted from refunds, or "submitting a claim doesn't guarantee approval".

## Cost to Karman

Worst case for a cohort: all six seats claim a full refund, which is one cohort's revenue. The
realistic case is one or two claims per cohort, mostly the half-refund kind, because the attendance
and practice rules filter out students who don't do the work.

This estimate was made for the flat "+100, or +50 from 1400" target. The tiered targets ask for more from
low baselines (+150 on the cohort and +200 on Private 20 from 1200 or lower), so half-refund claims there
are more likely than under +100. The cost has not been re-estimated for the tiers.
