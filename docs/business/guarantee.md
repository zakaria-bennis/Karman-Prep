# The Karman guarantee (proposed, 2026-10-25)

Built from the market research session of 2026-10 (guarantee fine print of Prep Expert, PrepScholar,
Princeton Review, Kaplan, Revolution Prep, Magoosh and 1500 Blueprint, read from their own terms pages).
It replaces "+50 points or your next cohort is free". **Status: proposal. A lawyer must read the final
terms before they go on the site.** The numbers live in `src/lib/marketing/offer.ts` (`GUARANTEE`).

## Why it changed

- +50 points with a free seat as the only remedy was the weakest headline in the market. Competitors
  advertise +150 to +200 with money back, but pay for those numbers with fine print (100% of homework,
  poll quotas, the same Zoom account, "good faith", "submitting doesn't guarantee approval").
- Families punish vague rules in reviews. Princeton Review's guarantee is the most complained-about on
  the market; a consumer watchdog criticized its "1400+ Guaranteed" ad for hiding the starting-score rule.
- So Karman's guarantee is a strong promise with rules that are all numbers the family can check on the
  student's dashboard, printed next to the promise. Nothing in a separate terms page that changes it.

## The SAT Program (cohort)

**+100 points over your baseline, or a 1450. If not, you choose: money back or the next cohort free.**

| Official SAT result | What the family gets |
|---|---|
| +100 or more, or 1450 and up | Nothing back. The promise was kept |
| +50 to +99 | Half the price back, or the next cohort free. The family chooses |
| Under +50 | The full price back, or the next cohort free. The family chooses |

**Who it covers**
- A student's first Karman program, once per student.
- Baseline: an official SAT or PSAT from the last 6 months. Without one, the week-1 Bluebook test,
  proctored live by Karman.
- Students starting at 1450 or above are not covered. Karman says so before they enroll.
- Paid in full, or every installment paid on time.

**What the student does** (every rule is a number shown on the student's dashboard)
- Attend at least 15 of the 18 live classes, live. A recording doesn't count.
- Complete all five one-on-one reviews. Rescheduling with 24 hours' notice is fine.
- Take all five full-length practice tests, each in one timed sitting, in the week it's assigned.
- Complete at least 90% of the assigned weekly practice.
- Sit the official SAT on the program's test date. If that SAT is cancelled, or with a doctor's note,
  the next SAT within 60 days counts.

**How to claim**
- Within 30 days of scores coming out, email the official College Board score report to
  guarantee@karmanprep.com. One email. No mailed paper reports, no fees taken out of the refund.

**What Karman will not add:** a "good faith" clause, quotas on answering polls, a same-device rule,
fees subtracted from refunds, or "submitting a claim doesn't guarantee approval".

## Private 20 (20 hours, one-on-one)

**+100 points or a 1450.** Students starting at 1450 or above are not covered.
- The student attends every session. Cancelling with less than 24 hours' notice counts as a missed session.
- The student takes four full-length practice tests.
- The student sits the official SAT within 30 days of the last session.
- If the score falls short: up to 6 more hours free first. If the next official SAT still falls short,
  half the price back.

## No guarantee

- Private 10: ten hours is too few to promise a result. No competitor guarantees packages this small.
- The free practice test and any workshop-only access.

## Cost to Karman

Worst case for a cohort: all five paid seats claim a full refund, which is one cohort's revenue. The
realistic case is one or two claims per cohort, mostly the half-refund kind, because the attendance
and practice rules filter out students who don't do the work. Each free seat is about 20% of the next
cohort's paid seats, so letting the family choose between money and a seat costs about the same either way.
