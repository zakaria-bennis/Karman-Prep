new plan ladder (Free, Platform, Plus, cohort, Private 1:1, School).

Prices are list prices, mostly from the vendors' own pages, checked 2026-10-30.
They're before promotions; Magoosh and UWorld were both running 20% off. Any
number marked "unverified" came from a search snippet or a third-party site.
Re-check those before quoting any of them on the site.

## What a parent sees

- **Free.** Khan Academy plus Bluebook is official, costs nothing, and feels
  good enough to many parents. To get paid, Karman must give them what free
  can't: a plan, someone checking in, visible progress, and a guarantee.
- **Under $30 a month.** 1600.io and phone apps. Parents treat it as a cheap
  add-on and buy without much research.
- **$30 to $80 a month, or $100 to $400 one time.** Magoosh, OnePrep, UWorld,
  PrepScholar, Princeton Review, Kaplan. Most of these offer a guarantee, so
  parents expect one at this price and compare question counts and brands.
- **Live classes, $799 to $2,199.** Karman's $995 founding cohort sits in the
  middle.
- **1:1 tutoring.** Big brands charge about $175 to $200 an hour. Karman's
  $145 with the founder teaching is below them.

## Gaps Karman can own

- **Live help at software prices.** Live teaching usually starts near $800.
  Plus with weekly live workshops and office hours under $60 a month would be
  close to unique.
- **Parents can see the progress.** No consumer page reviewed advertises a
  parent dashboard or weekly parent report, and the parent is the buyer.
- **Daily review that sticks.** Only Achievable describes retention-based
  review, and only apps do streaks. Nobody combines a Daily 10 with a large
  original bank and live teaching.
- **The largest original bank.** 10,000 would beat PrepScholar (4,700+),
  Magoosh (2,500+), Piqosity (2,300+) and UWorld (2,000+). Lead with quality
  that feels like the real test, never "official".

## Suggested ranges from the research

- **Platform:** $12 to $19 a month. Sits beside 1600.io and the apps. 500
  questions is fewer than Achievable or Piqosity give for similar money, so
  the Daily 10, skill levels and rewards carry the value.
- **Plus:** $39 to $59 a month. Beside OnePrep Pro and Magoosh Premium, but
  with live workshops that Magoosh doesn't offer live at any price. Above
  about $59, the $995 cohort starts to look like a pricier Plus.
- **Schools:** $8 to $15 per student per year at Platform level, $15 to $25
  with the full bank and recordings, live sessions as a paid add-on. A free
  first-semester pilot for one or two schools opens the door.

Founder decisions so far (2026-10-30): plain monthly billing; no guarantee on
Free, Platform, Plus or School; the guarantee stays on cohort and Private.
Most paid self-study competitors do offer a guarantee, so Plus will compete
without one.
