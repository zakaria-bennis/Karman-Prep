# School pricing research (2026-10-30)

The founder wants to sell Karman to high schools cheaply, per student, mainly
to spread the product and undercut competitors. His candidate was $15 per
student a year with a free first semester. Prices below are per student per
year unless noted. "Unverified" means a secondary source.

## How schools buy

- **The term is usually a yearly license, July 1 to June 30.** Semester
  licenses are rare.
- **Budgets are set in spring and adopted in May or June.** Orders go out in
  July. Spring SAT School Day runs March 1 to April 30, 2027.
- **Small purchases need no formal bid.** Under federal rules, purchases below
  about $10,000 to $15,000 need no competing quotes. State limits vary (for
  example, $40,000 in Washington). A 400-student school at $15 is $6,000, which
  a principal can usually approve with a purchase order.
- **Buyers care about:**
  - no extra cost on top of SAT School Day
  - usage and growth reports
  - sign-in through Clever or ClassLink
  - a signed student data privacy agreement
  - little teacher time

## What this means for Karman

- **$15 doesn't undercut the market.** Khan, Bluebook and Varsity Tutors are
  free. Khan Districts ($8 to $10), MasteryPrep ($10) and possibly Method ($3)
  are cheaper. Albert and MasteryPrep all-access tie at $15. The site can't
  claim "cheapest" while free options exist (marketing honesty rules).
- **A free first semester is more generous than usual.**
  - Albert charges for its trial and credits it toward a purchase.
  - A school that never paid has no budget line to renew from.
  - A pilot that ends after May or June misses the budget year.
  - Each school brings privacy agreements, rostering, training and support,
    which is a lot for one person.
- **Selling to families through school accounts is restricted.** California
  (SOPIPA) and similar state laws ban using school student data for marketing.
  Family upgrades must be opt-in and started by the parent.

## Options to choose from

1. **Free core, paid school tools (the researcher's recommendation).**
   - Students and teachers get practice, Your Daily 10, tests, the calculator
     and a basic teacher view for free, forever.
   - Admin, seat keys, assignments, reports and parent accounts cost $5 per
     student a year, capped at $1,500 per school, on a July to June term.
   - Up to 10 founding schools get the paid tools free from January to June
     2027.
2. **$2 per student, everything included.** The lowest real paid price, with
   very little revenue and the most support per dollar.
3. **A flat $750 to $1,000 per school.** One invoice, an easy sign-off, no
   seat counting.
4. **$15 as planned.** Sell on the product rather than the price, with a
   60-day trial or a paid pilot credited toward the license.
