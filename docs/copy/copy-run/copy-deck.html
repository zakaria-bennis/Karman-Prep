The race

You can't start in the middle. Two telephone poles — the copy is the wire.

Point A — where they are
A parent of an 11th grader who has seen '+200 points guaranteed' ads, suspects the rules are buried on a terms page, has maybe paid for prep before and watched the homework stop after week two, and has no way to tell what is happening until the score arrives.
→
Point B — where they land
Karman shows the whole deal before I talk to anyone (price, dates, every rule of the guarantee as a number), it ends on my kid's test date, and I will see every week whether the work got done. Next step: book the free practice test on December 12.
A different approach
The usual SAT prep sells open-ended hours or subscriptions, prints a big guarantee and hides its rules on a separate page, then stops watching once you have paid. Karman sells a program that ends on one SAT date, prints every rule as a number, and reports the work to the parent every week.

Cut

  • 'The climb starts before sunrise.' Beautiful film line, but a parent can't falsify it and any brand could sign it.
  • '10,000+ original practice questions.' Live count was 2,588 on 2026-10-19; the release check blocks the claim. Written around until the bank reaches it.
  • 'We don't sell hours. We sell March 6.' Two-beat antithesis, banned by copy rule 16.
  • 'Cameras on, questions answered live.' No fact says cameras are required.
  • 'Our tutors know every name.' Talk, not a fact.

Read this before you spend money

  • Every specific in this copy traces to a fact you provided or a review you supplied — check them before you spend money behind them. This writes the copy; it doesn't verify your claims or clear them for compliance.
  • No customer reviews exist yet (honesty rule), so every line is built on offer facts and the 1:1 track record, not customer words. Once real reviews exist, re-run this with them.
  • The guarantee is a proposal waiting on a lawyer. The headline and guarantee section only stay true if the lawyer keeps the full terms on the page.
  • Class days and times are still marked CONFIRM in offer.ts.
  • The students map numbers (80 U.S., 20 abroad) are unconfirmed; the map copy avoids stating them until you confirm.
