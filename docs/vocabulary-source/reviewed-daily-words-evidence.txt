IMPORTANT LIMITS
- Source verification and review were AI-assisted editorial work, not independent human sign-off or College Board endorsement.
- Difficulty is editorial, not measured by student response data. Words such as esteem, earnest and latent provide shorter challenging rounds; long words are not automatically harder educationally.
- Sanction has conflicting senses; compound, manifest, operative, antecedent and other words have multiple parts of speech or meanings. Keep the supplied part-of-speech label and caution with the reveal.
- Exact American spellings are the answers. The game should not silently rotate in British spellings or inflections without additional records and stable IDs.
- SAT Words in Context tests contextual precision. This game supplements spelling and word familiarity; it is not a substitute for context-based SAT practice.
- No offensive slurs, proper names, hyphenations, spaces, isolated roots or niche technical-only forms were included. Negative-tone academic words such as misanthropic remain legitimate vocabulary.
