# Portal welcomes

Status: draft for the founder to review. 200 welcome lines for the student
portal, shown one at a time above the dashboard, in the same spirit as the
greetings in the Claude app. `{name}` is the student's first name. Lines are
picked by time of day, day of the week, class days and the weeks before the
test, and never repeat twice in a row.

Rules: no em dashes, no fake urgency, no jokes about studying, nothing that
sounds like a chatbot. A line without `{name}` is used when the name is
unknown.
