# Modern UI study, 1 November 2026

The founder looked at the three rebuilt home pages (`day`, `numbers`, `portal`
in `.impeccable/mocks/rebuild/`) and said they need to look more modern, with
the UI elements of new-era websites. This document says what makes them look
dated, lists the elements that make current sites look current, and ends with a
recipe for a modern Karman home page.

It does not repeat earlier research. Colour, words, motion tokens and eye
guidance are in `docs/archive/design/motion-and-colour-brief.md`. Imagery systems are in
`docs/archive/design/imagery-direction.md`. Scroll-driven motion patterns (pinned
question, camera push, marker behind a word, wordmark footer rising) are in
`docs/design/research/gallery-sweep-2026-11-01.md`. Where an element below
overlaps one of those, it says so and only adds what is new: the static build
(radius, border, type, timing).

Since 2026-11-01 the design rules are things to steer away from when unsure,
not hard bans (`docs/archive/design-rules.md`, "Founder decision, 2026-11-01"). Each
element below names the rule it bends and why that is acceptable. Contrast
(33), no overlapping text (34), no sideways scroll (28), no invented claims and
the copy rules still hold.

## How this was captured

- Playwright (Chromium, from the repo's `node_modules`) at 1440 x 900 and
  390 x 844, light colour scheme. Six desktop screens per site (scrolled one
  viewport at a time) and two phone screens. Cookie banners were declined where
  a decline button existed (Cursor, Granola). Brilliant offers only "Got it",
  so its banner stays in the shots. Nothing was signed into or submitted.
- A script then read the computed styles of the header, headings, buttons,
  pills, glass layers and the corner radius of every large surface. The
  numbers below come from that, not from eyeballing.
- Contact sheets (six desktop shots in a grid, two phone shots below) are in
  `docs/design/research/modern-ui-2026-11-01/<site>.jpg`. Full-size PNGs are in
  the session scratchpad, which is temporary:
  `/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-zakariabennis-Karman-Prep/eae5e585-9868-40a3-bd97-fb3ad26100f3/scratchpad/modern-ui/`
  (`<site>-1440-<n>.png`, `<site>-390-<n>.png`, `probe.json`).

## 1. What looks dated on the three Karman pages

Contact sheets: `modern-ui-2026-11-01/karman_day.jpg`,
`karman_numbers.jpg`, `karman_portal.jpg`.

### Shared across all three

These are the main reasons. They matter more than any page-specific detail.

1. **Every surface is square.** The style probe found no surface larger than
   260 x 160 px with a corner radius of 6 px or more on any of the three pages.
   Buttons are 4 px. On the reference sites, large surfaces cluster at 12 to
   28 px (Apple 28, Speak 24 to 40, Raycast 20, Resend 16 to 24, Brilliant 20
   to 34, Granola 16 to 28, Cal.com 12 to 16) and buttons are full pills
   (Granola, Cursor, Brilliant, ElevenLabs, Speak, Linear) or 8 to 10 px
   (Vercel, Notion, Attio). Square corners on photos and panels now read as
   2016 to 2019 editorial web.
2. **Offset colour slabs behind photos and screens.** A marigold, pine or rust
   rectangle sits behind or around each picture, shifted off its edge (day:
   hero, Daily 10, live class, parent; numbers: pine slab behind the hero,
   rust frame around the question; portal: pine panel under the hero text).
   It is a print poster move. No reference site frames media this way; they
   put media inside a rounded card, or float a product window over a picture.
3. **The header is a flat bar welded to the top edge.** All three use a
   full-width, opaque, bone bar 64 to 72 px tall with a rectangular marigold
   button. Seven of the references float the navigation as a rounded or pill
   container inset from the edges (Granola, Brilliant, Speak, Raycast, Cal.com,
   ElevenLabs on phones, Attio's glass layer).
4. **The product is shown flat.** The quiz screen appears at small size inside a
   thick coloured frame, with no device, no window chrome, no depth and no
   light. Cursor, Granola, Speak, Superhuman, Attio and Notion all show their
   product as a crisp window or phone floating over a picture or a soft light
   field, large enough to read.
5. **No containers.** Text and pictures sit straight on the page ground, with
   wide empty bone areas between them (day, fifth and seventh shots; numbers,
   second shot). Current sites group content into cards and panels that sit on
   a slightly different tone from the page, so the page reads as a set of
   objects, not a printed sheet.
6. **Display type is heavy.** Archivo at weight 760 (numbers) and Bricolage
   Grotesque at 700 (day) read as sports poster. The references set display
   type between 400 and 600 (Vercel 400, Linear 510, Superhuman 460, Notion
   600, Cal.com 600, Granola 400 serif) with very tight tracking.
7. **Plans are table rows.** Day and portal show plans as grey bars or a
   comparison table. The founder already approved tier cards for pricing
   (Paddle reference), and every reference shows plans as cards.

### Day (`karman_day.jpg`)

- The clock idea is strong ("6:50 a.m.", "7:40 a.m."), but each time sits in a
  corner far from its picture, with large empty areas between (fifth screen,
  "9:15 p.m.": the headline is alone at the bottom right).
- Pictures are cropped hard into marigold, pine and rust slabs that bleed off
  the page edge. It is the most "poster" of the three.
- A ghosted heading ("Next fall") is cut off by the section edge in the seventh
  shot. It reads as a bug in a still.
- The footer wordmark is the most modern thing on the page. Keep it.

### Numbers (`karman_numbers.jpg`)

- Huge condensed digits in marigold ("1570", "18", "2 7 21", "+100", "Dec 12")
  make the page feel like a scoreboard. Big numbers are a current element (see
  stat strip, below), but here every section uses one, so none stands out.
- The rust frame around the question and the pine frame around the hero photo
  are thick borders, not cards.
- The footer wordmark repeats unchanged across the last three screens because
  the closing section is short. The page ends on a large flat word with no
  container.

### Portal (`karman_portal.jpg`)

- Literata serif headings on pine panels feel bookish and older than the
  others.
- The pine text panel over the hero photo is a solid rectangle. Current sites
  would make it a rounded card, or a glass card that lets the photo through.
- Plans are a table with a pine column. Readable, but it looks like a 2015
  comparison grid.
- The results odometer, captured mid-roll in the sixth shot, shows digits
  sliced in half next to the label. Check that the roll cannot overlap the
  label at any scroll step (rule 34).
- The closing "hello@karmanprep.com" headline is a nice idea that the
  references also use (big type in the footer), but it sits on flat bone with
  a thin row of links.

## 2. Catalogue: 18 elements that signal "new era"

Each entry: where it was seen (contact sheet in `modern-ui-2026-11-01/`), how it
is built (measured where possible), how Karman should use it, the best
21st.dev component to port (metadata only; recolour with Karman tokens and
credit the id in a comment), and which rule it bends.

Karman tokens used below: ink `#12110f`, bone `#f5f5f2`, card `#eceae3`,
marigold `#eba21f`, pine `#1f4d45`, dark ground `#151412`, dark ink `#f1ede4`.

### 2.1 Floating pill navigation

- **Seen:** Granola (`granola.jpg`), Brilliant (`brilliant.jpg`), Speak
  (`speak.jpg`), Raycast (`raycast.jpg`), Cal.com (`cal.jpg`).
- **Built:** an inset container, not a full-width bar. Brilliant: 1176 x 55 px,
  26 px from the top, radius 9999, white, 1 px `#e4e4e4` border, no shadow.
  Granola: 1217 x 56 px, 8 px from the top, radius 9999, links as 38 px pills
  that get a tint on hover (0.2 s, `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)`). Speak:
  1392 x 68 px, 20 px from the top, radius 32, `backdrop-filter: blur(25px)`.
  Raycast: a dark inset box with a 1 px white-6% border. On phones it stays a
  pill with a 16 px side gap.
- **Karman:** a pill 56 px tall, max width 1180 px, 12 px from the top. Bone at
  92% opacity with `blur(20px)`, 1 px ink-at-8% border, radius 9999. Wordmark
  left, four links centre (15 px), one marigold pill button right. It hides
  when scrolling down and returns when scrolling up (200 ms translate), so it
  never sits on text being read.
- **21st.dev:** `26786` Elevate Navbar (glass pill, reduced motion respected;
  drop its dropdown panels). Simpler base: `18090` Floating Navbar.
- **Bends:** rule 8 (glass) lightly, rule 34 (sticky over text). Acceptable
  because the blur is a quiet frosted layer, not liquid glass, and the hide on
  scroll down keeps it off reading text. Run the rule 34 text-box scan.

### 2.2 Announcement pill above the headline

- **Seen:** Granola ("New" chip + "Voice typing by Granola"), Cal.com
  ("Cal.com launches v6.9.5"), Attio, Resend, ElevenLabs (bar version).
- **Built:** 24 to 32 px tall, radius 9999, ground one step off the page
  (Cal.com `#f5f5f5`, Granola `#f7f7f2`), 12 to 14 px text, chevron at the end,
  padding about `4px 8px 4px 12px`. Cal.com adds `0 4px 8px rgba(34,42,53,0.05)`.
  Granola puts a small coloured "New" chip inside.
- **Karman:** one pill over the hero headline that carries the nearest real
  date, for example the free practice test date from `offer.ts`. Card tone
  `#eceae3` in light, `#1e1c19` in dark, ink text, a small pine dot, chevron.
  It is a text link, not a second highlighted button (rule 35), so no marigold
  fill.
- **21st.dev:** `983` Hero Pill (originui).
- **Bends:** nothing. Watch rule 35 and keep it unfilled.

### 2.3 Large rounded media cards

- **Seen:** Apple (`apple.jpg`, 28 px cards on `#f5f5f7`), Speak (40 px card,
  56 px padding, `0 0 50px rgba(56,56,56,0.1)`), Brilliant, Granola, Cursor
  (product windows on painted backdrops inside rounded cards).
- **Built:** radius 24 to 40 px for section-size cards, 16 to 20 px for
  smaller ones. Inner content padded 40 to 56 px. Depth comes from a tone step
  (card a few points darker or lighter than the page), not a shadow. Apple uses
  no shadow at all. Speak's shadow is a 50 px blur at 10%, the softest seen.
- **Karman:** every photo and product picture goes inside a card: 28 px radius
  on desktop, 20 px on phones, `overflow: hidden`. Replace every offset slab
  with this. Colour moves from the slab to the card ground (a pine or marigold
  card is still allowed, within section 2.2 of the motion-and-colour brief).
- **21st.dev:** none needed; plain CSS.
- **Bends:** nothing if depth comes from tone. Rule 5 only if a shadow is
  added; use Speak's level at most.

### 2.4 Framed hero stage

- **Seen:** Cal.com (`cal.jpg`): the whole hero sits in a 1176 x 700 card,
  radius 12, on a grey page, with a hairline shadow
  (`0 1px 5px -4px rgba(36,36,36,0.7), 0 4px 8px rgba(36,36,36,0.05)`).
  ElevenLabs and Apple do the same with a tinted card.
- **Built:** page ground one step darker than the card; hero card inset 16 to
  24 px from the viewport edge, radius 12 to 28.
- **Karman:** the hero becomes one rounded stage on the card tone, holding the
  headline on the left and the iPhone on the right. It makes the first screen
  read as one object, which is the opposite of today's loose layout.
- **21st.dev:** none; CSS.
- **Bends:** nothing.

### 2.5 Bento grid with live product tiles

- **Seen:** Notion (`notion.jpg`), Superhuman (`superhuman.jpg`), ElevenLabs
  (`elevenlabs.jpg`), Attio (`attio.jpg`).
- **Built:** a 12-column grid with 12 to 16 px gaps. Tiles have radius 12 to 16
  (Notion: 8 px small tiles, tinted ground such as `#fef3f1`, 1 px
  black-at-5% border, 16 px padding). Tile sizes are uneven: one wide tile
  with a real screen, two or three narrow ones. Each tile is a short label
  (14 px), one line of body, and a real piece of product UI, never an icon and
  three lines of text. Superhuman fills tiles with a photo-gradient backdrop
  and a floating product card.
- **Karman:** one bento of four tiles under the hero: Your Daily 10 (wide, the
  real question screen), the calendar (next live class), the parent update
  (weekly email card), and the live class on Zoom (student and tutor tiles).
  Ground `#eceae3` in light, `#1e1c19` in dark, radius 20, gap 14.
- **21st.dev:** `622` Bento Grid (kokonutd), or `9206` Bento Product Features.
- **Bends:** rule 13 (bento grids) and rule 6 (three cards in a row).
  Acceptable because tiles are uneven and each shows a real Karman screen, which
  is what separates a current bento from the generated "icon plus three lines"
  kind the rule was written against.

### 2.6 Feature tabs that swap a device screen

- **Seen:** Superhuman (Featured / Prep for meetings / Write and edit / ...,
  underline tabs above a card row), ElevenLabs (Creative / Agents / API inside
  a rounded card), Raycast (icon dock that swaps the screen above it),
  Brilliant (Math / Coding).
- **Built:** a row of three to five tabs. Active tab gets a sliding background
  pill (radius 9999) or a 2 px underline. The screen above cross-fades
  (150 to 250 ms) with a small 8 to 12 px rise. Keyboard arrows move between
  tabs.
- **Karman:** four tabs under the iPhone: Daily 10, Calendar, Live class,
  Parent update. Clicking swaps the phone screen. This is how the iPhone shows
  all four real screens without four sections.
- **21st.dev:** `27081` Feature Tab Switcher (vertical list beside a panel,
  keyboard-ready), `26923` Segmented Tabs (sliding background).
- **Bends:** nothing. Tabs are clicked, not filled by scroll (rule 37 is safe).

### 2.7 Segmented control and audience switch

- **Seen:** Brilliant ("I'm a learner" / "I'm a parent or teacher" under the
  headline; Foundations / Algebra / Calculus chips), Cal.com (15m / 30m / 45m /
  1h inside the booking widget).
- **Built:** Brilliant's chips are 46 px tall, radius 9999, 20 px side padding,
  active is black with `0 4px 10px rgba(0,0,0,0.05)` and
  `transform 0.25s cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`. Cal.com's segments are
  28 px tall, radius 6, inside a grey track.
- **Karman:** a "For parents / For students" switch near the bento, which
  changes the tile captions and the phone screen to the parent or student
  view. Also the monthly / yearly switch on pricing, if one is ever needed.
- **21st.dev:** `34935` Segmented Control (balick, sliding pill), or `34763`
  (halaska-studio, iOS style).
- **Bends:** nothing.

### 2.8 Display type: medium weight, tight tracking

- **Seen:** every reference. Measured headline sizes and tracking at 1440:
  Vercel 64 px / weight 400 / -3.84 px (-0.06 em); Notion 96 px / 600 /
  -4.6 px (-0.048 em); Resend section heads 56 px / 400 / -2.8 px (-0.05 em);
  Linear 64 px / 510 / -1.41 px (-0.022 em); Superhuman 49 px / 460 (a
  variable font at an in-between weight) / -1.35 px; Granola 86 px serif /
  400 / -1.73 px; Attio 64 px / 600 / -1.28 px.
- **Built:** weight 400 to 600, tracking -0.02 to -0.06 em, line height 1.0 to
  1.1, one size for the hero (56 to 96 px) and one for sections (40 to 56 px).
  Variable fonts allow in-between weights such as 460 or 510.
- **Karman:** hero 72 px desktop / 44 px phone, weight 560, tracking -0.04 em,
  line height 1.02. Section heads 48 / 32 px, weight 540, -0.03 em. See
  section 3 for faces.
- **21st.dev:** not a component.
- **Bends:** nothing.

### 2.9 Two-tone headline

- **Seen:** Linear ("A new species of product tool." in white, the rest in
  grey), Attio, Superhuman ("Save 4+ hours every week" dark, "on email and
  scheduling" grey), Speak ("Three steps" dark, "to spoken fluency" faded).
- **Built:** one heading, same size and weight; the second clause is a span in
  ink at about 45 to 60%.
- **Karman:** for a headline plus its support line set as one block, the
  support line in ink at 62% (`#686865` on bone, 5.12:1; dark mode ink at
  55%, `#8e8b86` on `#151412`, 5.42:1). Both pass AA for any size.
- **21st.dev:** none; a span.
- **Bends:** nothing, but copy rule 16 (no two-beat antithesis) still applies,
  so the grey half must add a fact, not a contrast.

### 2.10 One highlighted word as a chip

- **Seen:** Notion ("Ship" set inside a green pill with a dot, inline in a
  96 px headline), Resend ("this weekend" as gradient text), Granola ("New"
  chip).
- **Built:** an inline span with radius 9999 (or 0.3 em), horizontal padding
  0.15 to 0.25 em, background in the accent, text in ink. Notion's chip carries
  a small dot. Resend uses `background-clip: text` with a gradient.
- **Karman:** one marigold chip per page at most, on the key word of the hero
  ("ten questions a day", "comes back"). Ink on marigold is 8.74:1. The
  gallery sweep's pattern 8 is the animated version (marker sweeps in); this
  is the static look it lands on.
- **21st.dev:** `12045` Marker Highlight, or `28151` Text Highlight.
- **Bends:** skip gradient text (rules 1 and 4). The chip bends nothing.

### 2.11 Product window or phone floating over a picture

- **Seen:** Cursor (app windows over landscape paintings, inside rounded
  cards), Granola (notes window and Zoom tiles over art), Speak (a phrase card
  over a photo of people), Superhuman (UI over photo gradients).
- **Built:** the backdrop is a full-bleed picture inside a 16 to 28 px card.
  The product sits on it as a window (radius 10 to 12, 1 px light border) or a
  phone, with a soft shadow, scaled so its text is readable.
- **Karman:** the iPhone 17 Pro Max (rule 58: 440 x 956 points, thin bezel,
  Dynamic Island) over a generated photo of a student, inside a rounded card.
  This is imagery system C in `imagery-direction.md`; what is new here is the
  card and the readable scale (phone at least 360 px tall on desktop).
- **21st.dev:** `6475` Iphone Mockup as a base, redrawn to 17 Pro Max
  proportions.
- **Bends:** rule 5 (a soft shadow under the phone). Acceptable at about
  `0 30px 60px -20px rgba(18,17,15,0.25)`, which reads as a real object on a
  surface rather than a decoration.

### 2.12 Soft light field and fine grain

- **Seen:** Attio (lavender light behind the hero product, frosted windows
  with `blur(12px)` at 80%), Linear (a faint glow under the hero screen),
  Raycast (red light behind the hero), Resend (a dark glow at the top of each
  section), Cursor (painted backdrops with grain).
- **Built:** a radial gradient, ellipse at 50% 0% or behind the product, at 8
  to 15% opacity, fading to the ground with no visible edge. Grain is an SVG
  `feTurbulence` noise layer at 3 to 5% opacity, `mix-blend-mode: multiply`
  in light and `screen` in dark.
- **Karman:** a warm marigold field at 10% behind the phone in light mode; a
  pine field at 18% in dark mode. Grain at 4% over the whole ground.
- **21st.dev:** `17835` Grain Gradient (paper-design shader). It is WebGL and
  heavy; prefer the CSS version and credit the idea.
- **Bends:** rules 1 (harsh gradients) and 21 (radial orbs). Acceptable only
  while it stays a field with no visible circle edge. If you can point at the
  orb, it is too strong.

### 2.13 Spotlight cards on hover

- **Seen:** Linear (feature cards), Raycast (extension cards). Desktop pointer
  only.
- **Built:** a radial gradient about 300 px wide follows the pointer inside the
  card at 6 to 10% opacity, and the 1 px border brightens where the pointer
  is. Updates through CSS variables `--x` and `--y`, no layout work.
- **Karman:** on the bento tiles and plan cards, marigold at 8%. Off on touch
  and under reduced motion.
- **21st.dev:** `35180` Spotlight (kevingirelli, soft light plus lit edge).
- **Bends:** rule 21 lightly; it is a pointer light, not a decorative orb.

### 2.14 Stat strip in a hairline grid (instead of a logo marquee)

- **Seen:** Cursor (logo tiles in a grid of cells), Attio and Superhuman (logos

- **Built:** a row of equal cells divided by 1 px hairlines (ink at 8 to 10%),
  each 72 to 120 px tall, content centred. Marquees, where used, fade at the
  edges with a mask and pause on hover.
- **Karman:** Karman has no customer logos and must not invent any, and

  16 weeks") with a small portrait, and the next class date. One row, no
  scrolling.
- **21st.dev:** none for the grid. If a marquee is ever wanted: `23537` Logo
  Marquee (pauses on hover and focus, respects reduced motion).
- **Bends:** nothing. A marquee would need care with rule 28 (no sideways
  scroll); a masked, contained one does not scroll the page.

### 2.15 Tactile pill buttons

- **Seen:** Linear ("Sign up": radius 9999, 1 px border, four stacked tiny

### 2.16 Modern FAQ accordion

### 2.17 Big wordmark footer inside a card

### 2.18 Glass bottom bar (sticky action after the hero)

- **Seen:** Arc (a bottom bar "Moving to Dia is easy" with a dark pill button),

- **21st.dev:** `5841` LumaBar for the material, or `32111` Glass Pagination.

## 3. Type recommendation

The reference sites mostly use custom or paid faces (CursorGothic, Super Sans
VF, Cal Sans, Waldenburg, quadrant, ABC Favorit). The free faces closest to
that look, avoiding Inter, Geist, Space Grotesk, Instrument Sans and Fraunces,
and also avoiding what the three mocks already used (Bricolage Grotesque,
Archivo, Literata, Albert Sans, Hanken Grotesk, IBM Plex Sans):

1. **Funnel Display + Funnel Sans** (Google Fonts, variable 300 to 800).
   Recommended. Display has the slightly quirky, confident shapes of Cal Sans
   and CursorGothic without being a copy; Sans is a calm text partner from the
   same family. Use Display at 540 to 580 with -0.04 em for headings, Sans at
   400 to 500 for body and UI.
2. **Host Grotesk** (Google Fonts, variable 300 to 800). One family for
   everything, in the Superhuman and Attio lane: clean, neutral, works at
   in-between weights like 460 and 520. Safest choice if Funnel feels too
   characterful.
3. **Newsreader** (Google Fonts, variable weight 200 to 800, optical size 6 to
   72), as an accent only. Granola, Brilliant and Resend all pair a soft serif
   with a sans. Use Newsreader italic for one phrase per page (for example the
   parent line), never for body, so the page does not turn bookish the way
   portal's Literata did.

All three were checked live on the Google Fonts CSS API on 1 November 2026.
Self-host the woff2 files when building.
