# The scroll film: storyboard

Replaces the still-photo plan. The homepage tells one story as you scroll, the
way the Jesko Jets and Lando Norris sites do: full-screen moving pictures that
advance with the scroll, and large kinetic type that carries the message. The
product itself is shown as real, sharp interface built in code and laid over
the film where a feature is named. It is never pasted into a generated picture.

## Rules for the film

1. People in every chapter except the first and last sky shots (rule 41).
2. Screens are never the subject of a generated shot. A face lit by a screen,
   a phone seen from the side, a laptop seen from behind: the light tells you
   they are studying. The real Karman screens appear beside the film, in code.
3. Every shot obeys physics: a screen faces the person using it.
4. One cast throughout, from the approved portraits (Ava, Clara, Mateo, Julian,
   Priya, Ethan, David, two parents).
5. Kinetic text: one short line per chapter, set very large, that rises,
   tracks or splits as the chapter scrolls in. No text over text (rule 34),
   no scroll-filling bars (rule 37), no play buttons (rule 38).
