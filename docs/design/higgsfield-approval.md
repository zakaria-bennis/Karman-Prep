## A. Ground rules for every picture

1. People first: students of 16 and 17, then parents and tutors. They are
   focused, with at most a small smile; nobody laughs at a screen.
2. They study on phones and laptops, not paper. AirPods Pro or AirPods Max are
   allowed. No visible logos on devices, clothes or bags, and no physical
   calculators.
3. Every screen is generated blank white. We add the real Karman screen
   afterwards, so every word on it is one you approved (section D).
4. Families are well off, and settings and clothes reflect that quietly.
5. The look is documentary: natural skin, true colour and real light, with no
   teal-and-orange grade, haze or flare. Colour is free; it only has to look
   like one shoot.
6. First names appear only as small film captions, never next to a score,
   quote or result.
