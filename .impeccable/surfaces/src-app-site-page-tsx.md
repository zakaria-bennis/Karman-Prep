# Home page (`/`)

Scope: the marketing home page. Mode: Persuade. Visitor: a parent of a high-school student deciding whether to pay for SAT prep. Action: Start free (sign up, land in the free student portal). Proof on hand: +280 average gain (1:1 tutoring, median 16 weeks), students at all top 12 US universities (30 logos), the tiered guarantee in `offer.ts`, the real portal. No testimonials. No AI-generated images. Tutors are shown with open-licence hand-drawn illustrations. Feeling the founder chose: Studio Ghibli (hand-painted, soft, quietly magical). He tried Duolingo-style chunky orange first and found it childish.

Order the founder approved on 2026-11-09 (he pinned this; it replaced a dealt hand of three single-device structures he rejected as "each one gimmick"):

1. The claim. "Our average student gains 280 points. Yours doesn't have to be average." Start free.
2. The proof. "We're twelve for twelve." The universities' logos on lit lanterns that rise, bump and come round again (settled 2026-11-10).
3. The offer. "Up to +200 points, or your money back." One sentence, the founder's "Karman" signature, a +200 stamp that lands, three outcome cards from the side; the rules are behind the link (settled 2026-11-10).
4. Who. "Our tutors are overqualified. That's the point."
5. How. "Six ways we make a skill stick, because one would be lazy."
6. Their doubts. "Go on. Ask us the hard ones."
7. The price and the close. "Four ways in. The first one is free." Start free.

He asked for a fun animation in every section and every transition (fifteen are listed in `prototypes/HANDOVER-home-2026-11-09.md`), built and approved one section at a time.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One sales argument told across one painted day. The page is a walk through Karman's own landscape from dawn to night, and each stop makes one point of the pitch. It refuses the category's stack of white cards on a white page.

OWN-WORLD: Karman's code-made painting behind everything, travelled by scroll. Words sit straight on the scene, on a soft edgeless band of mist, never in a box. Ink `#1d1a22` on cream mist by day; cream on indigo mist at night. Every action is ink, never a bright colour: the painting is the colour. Rounded, soft-shadowed shapes that settle when pressed. The headline's number is a solid ink shape with its lettering cut out, so the painting shows through. Young Serif for headlines, Hanken Grotesk for everything else and all numerals, Caveat for the rare handwritten aside.

STORY: The parent reads a result, sees where those students went, learns the exact score Karman will guarantee for their own child, meets who teaches and how, gets their doubts answered, sees the price, and starts free.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar as words on the sky, with a soft veil of light behind it that fades out below, and Start free in ink at the right. Headline at the left, about 76px, three lines, with "280 points" as a tilted ink shape whose lettering is cut out to the sky. The headline comes into focus out of the morning haze, word by word, then that shape slams in. One sentence of support in semibold ink, Start free (ink, with a breathing ring of the sky's light) and See pricing beneath, one line on what free includes. The right half is open dawn sky where two or three small flocks of birds cross and are gone. Treeline along the bottom. (Settled with the founder on 2026-11-10.)

FORM: A scroll-travelled painted scene with seven stops; user-pinned sequence, not a dealt structure. seed:7dcbb546f97dcdea.
