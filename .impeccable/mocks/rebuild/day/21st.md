# 21st.dev components in this page

Each one was fetched from 21st.dev, ported to plain HTML, CSS and GSAP (no React, no framer-motion),
recoloured with Karman's tokens, and credited in a comment where it is used.

| Id | Name (author) | Where | What was kept, what was dropped |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 18090 | Floating Navbar (arihantcodes) | The floating pill nav (`.bar` in `day.css`, "floating nav" in `day.js`) | Kept the inset pill container. Dropped the Lucide icon buttons, the dropdown and `shadow-lg`. Now a 56 px pill, 12 px from the top, bone at 90% with a 20 px blur and a 1 px ink-at-10% border; text links; hides going down and returns going up (200 ms). |
| 983 | Hero Pill (originui) | The pill above the hero headline (`.pill-link`) | Kept the rounded pill with a leading mark and a trailing chevron. The star icon became a small pine dot; the shadow was dropped for a 1 px hairline. It is a text link, never filled marigold (rule 35). |
| 26923 | Segmented Tabs (micka_design) | The day's tabs under the hero iPhone (`.tabs`, "the hero phone and the day's tabs" in `day.js`) | Kept the sliding selected background measured from the active tab and arrow-key activation. Dropped Base UI, framer-motion springs and the fluid hover layer; the slide is a CSS transform, 0.25 s, cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1). On tablets and phones the tabs sit in a 3-column grid and the indicator follows into the second row. |
| 35180 | Spotlight (kevingirelli) | The plan cards (`.spot`) | Kept the pointer-following radial light driven by two CSS variables per frame, skipped on touch. Marigold at 10%, 300 px. Dropped the lit edge ring. Off under reduced motion. |
| 35508 | Giant Wordmark Footer (kedhareswer) | The footer card (`.foot-card`, `.foot-word`) | Kept the idea: links in small type above a giant wordmark cropped by the card's bottom edge. Dropped the procedural alphabet, the disc and slab backdrop, the weight lens and the back-to-top key; the wordmark is Karman's own monoline drawing on a pine card. |
| 20068 | Count Up (unlumen) | The three results (`.r-score`, "Count" in `day.js`) | Kept the "slide" digit effect: one column of 0-9 per place moved by a transform, with a repeated 0 so the wrap is seamless. Dropped the spring, blur and fade. Each score rolls once, right to left, 0.06 s apart, 1.2 s. Screen readers hear the number once. |
| 18352 | Line Draw SVG (pulkitxm) | The keyword underline under each chapter headline (`landKw` in `day.js`) and the opening's drawn mark | Kept the stroke dash-offset draw, once. `pathLength="1"` replaces `getTotalLength()`. Never scrubbed by scroll. Colour per chapter (ink on marigold, marigold on pine and night, pale marigold on clay). |

Looked at and not used here:

- 26786 Elevate Navbar: 18090 is the simpler base; the glass came from the modern-UI study's measured values.
- 27081 Feature Tab Switcher: a vertical list beside a panel. The day reads left to right as times, so the
  horizontal segmented control fits better.
- 622 Bento Grid: the chapters already show each feature at full size in its own colour block, so a bento
  would show the same four screens twice.
