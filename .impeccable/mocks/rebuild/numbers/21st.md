# 21st.dev components used on the Numbers page

| Id | Name | Author | Where | How it was ported |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 20068 | Count Up | unlumen | Every big number: the tab numbers (10, 60, 39, 4), 6, the three scores, +100, Dec 12 | Its odometer (one clipped column per digit, 0 to 9 stacked, moved by `y`) rebuilt in plain JS + GSAP instead of React + motion springs. One turn then the digit; columns roll right to left 0.06 s apart, 1.2 s, the brief's `settle` ease; plays once per screen, and again when a tab is chosen. Digits are drawn from `data-d` by CSS so screen readers read the number once from `aria-label`. Credited in `numbers.js`. |
| 26923 | Segmented Tabs | micka_design | The four feature tabs on the pine card | The pill track with a sliding selected background, arrow keys that move and select, Home and End. Rebuilt without Base UI and framer-motion: plain buttons with `role="tab"`, the indicator moved by GSAP (0.25 s, `power3.inOut`). The hover highlight was left out. Recoloured: track black at 22 % on pine, indicator bone, active label ink. Credited in `index.html`. |
| 18090 | Floating Navbar | arihantcodes | The navigation | The inset pill with round controls and one filled action, made fixed at the top and hidden while scrolling down. No Lucide icons, no dropdown. Bone or dark card at 82 to 86 % with a 20 px blur. Credited in `index.html`. |
| 18352 | Line Draw SVG | pulkitxm | The opening: the logo's marigold line and the arc draw themselves | Stroke-dasharray = path length, dash offset tweened to 0 with GSAP, without React or ScrollTrigger; plays once on first visit. Credited in `index.html` and `numbers.js`. |
