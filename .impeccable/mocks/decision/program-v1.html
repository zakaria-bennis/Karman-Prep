Who gets a seat

Six seats means we choose carefully, and we say so when the program won't help.

We say yes when your student

  • can make both evening classes every week
  • will sit all five full-length tests
  • is aiming at one specific SAT date

We say no when

  • your student already scores 1500 or higher
  • the test is less than four weeks away

The 50-point guarantee

If your student's official SAT isn't at least 50 points above the week-one baseline, the next cohort is free. Your student attends at least 80% of classes and every review, sits all five tests, and takes the SAT on the program's date.

How a seat is offered

Your student applies in about three minutes. Zakaria reads it and calls you for twenty minutes. If the program fits, a $100 deposit holds the seat, and it's refundable.

One seat in every cohort is a scholarship seat, free for a student whose family can't afford the program. Write to us about it.
