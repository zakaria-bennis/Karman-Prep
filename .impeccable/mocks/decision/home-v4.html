TransitionsReading and Writing

A missed question, reviewed

Marine biologist Sylvia Earle led the first team of women aquanauts in 1970, living for two weeks in an underwater habitat off the U.S. Virgin Islands. ______ she set a record for the deepest untethered walk on the ocean floor.

Which choice completes the text with the most logical transition?

AIn other words,
BFor instance,
CLater,Right answer
DAs a result,Their choice
  • What it testsThe order of events. The record came after the habitat, and nothing says one caused the other.
  • Why D looked rightTwo achievements in a row feel connected, so a cause word sounds natural.
  • The fixSay how the two sentences relate in your own words first, then pick the transition that names it.

TransitionsReading and Writing

In 1938, chemist Roy Plunkett opened a cylinder of gas he planned to use in refrigerant research and found that it had turned into a slick white powder. Testing showed that the material, later sold as Teflon, was attacked by almost no chemical it touched. ______ its first large-scale use was not in kitchens: during World War II, it lined equipment that handled some of the most corrosive compounds of the Manhattan Project.

Which choice completes the text with the most logical transition?

The material resisted almost every chemical, so it was used where corrosive compounds had to be contained. The last sentence follows from the one before it, which calls for Accordingly. Even so would signal a contrast the text never sets up.
