Ranking

  1. Two Planes. The cleanest drawing here: it stays a crisp K at 16px, the fold is a quiet idea you can own, and as folded paper it is the best object of the set.
  2. Level A. The most premium on a page. A drawn wordmark with classical contrast reads like a house, not an app. Its K alone is plain, so it leans on the name.
  3. Keystone. The best story for families (the stone that holds the arch) and a solid favicon, but the flat mark is heavy next to Source Serif.

The Point is clear but looks like many tech marks. The Seal reads established but generic. The Date is the most literal and feels like an app icon.
