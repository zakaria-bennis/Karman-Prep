Karman logo, round 3: the K and the Kármán line

Built the way Allan Peters works: brand nouns first, then each mark combines the K, the Kármán line and one more noun, using one of his five tools (negative space, overlapping geometry, visual rhythm, an unexpected twist, visual flow). Black and white only, so colour does not decide it. Pick a gut top three.

01Open Sky

horizon, threshold

Overlap twist: solid below the line, only an outline above it. Past the line the K is open sky.

02Thin Air

atmosphere, altitude

Visual rhythm: above the line the K turns into stripes that thin out, the way air does with height.

48, 24, 16px

03Contrail

contrail, trajectory

Visual flow: the arm launches at the line and leaves three trails above it.

04Apogee

trajectory, apogee

Unexpected twist: the arm is a launch path that climbs through the line and levels off at the top.

05Planet Edge

planet, atmosphere

Overlapping geometry: the K stands over the curve of the earth; where they overlap the colours swap, and a thin arc above is the line.

06Lifted
