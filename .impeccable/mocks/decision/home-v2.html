What parents ask before they enroll

What does it cost?

The March Program is $995 for the founding cohort, or three monthly payments of $345. Private tutoring starts at $1,450 for 10 hours. See pricing

What if my student's score doesn't go up?

If the official score isn't at least 50 points above the week-1 baseline, the next cohort is free, as long as the student did the work. See the guarantee terms

Is this right for my student?

If your student already scores 1500 or higher, or the test is less than four weeks away, we'll tell you honestly that a program probably isn't the best use of your money.

When are the classes?

Tuesday and Thursday evenings, 90 minutes each, live on Zoom, from January 4.

What if we can't make those times?

Private 1:1 is scheduled around your student. See private tutoring
