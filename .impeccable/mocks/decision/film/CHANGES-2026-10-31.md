## What changed, page by page

### Every page
- Theme (rule 33): the inline head script reads the remembered choice (`karman-theme`) first, else the device,
  and is dark when the device states no preference. The toggle in the header and the phone menu overrides and
  remembers; without a remembered choice the page keeps following the device while open. The dark tokens still
  apply through `html[data-theme="dark"]`, so no flash of the wrong mode.
  One browser fact: browsers report `prefers-color-scheme: light` when a device has no setting, so "no
  preference" reaches the page as light. The page is dark whenever the device does not say light.
- Header: solid paper or solid black, never glass (rule 8); it slides away while reading down and comes back
  on the way up or when the menu opens, so it never sits on a line being read (rule 34).
- No eyebrow labels anywhere (the renderers ignore the empty `eyebrow:` lines).
- Shadows: every text shadow, drawn shadow plate (the Daily 10 drawing, the five page drawings) and soft glow
  is gone; the rings that marked chosen cards are 2px borders (rule 5). The phone's bright inner edge is drawn
  inset; the drawn iPhone is one interface fragment (`data-ui`).
- Hover (rule 25): the pointer-nearness layer is gone (phones, laptops, drawings, cards and photos no longer
  lean, light up or drift toward the cursor); the phone no longer turns on hover; arrows no longer slide.
  Hover stays on real controls only (nav underline, outlined button fill, button colour, link underline).
  The time-based glints the founder asked for stay (button, big marigold numbers, the orange phone edge, the
  facts' figures, the big prices).
- Lines: lists that were rows divided by 1px lines are now rows on a soft field with the paper showing
  between them (the guarantee's outcomes and obligations, the inner pages' label rows, steps, FAQ questions,
  statements). Real tables and form fields keep their lines.
- Every tap target is at least 44 by 44 (footer links, the pay toggle, the layout switch, the Private 20 mark).
- Favicon: `favicon.svg`, a K mark drawn in code. Open Graph: `og:title`, `og:description`, `og:image`,
  `og:url`, `og:type` and `twitter:card` in every head; the images are drawn in code by `og.mjs` into
  `og/<page>.png` (1200x630, paper, the K mark, the page's title and description). The real site serves them at
  `https://karmanprep.com/og/<page>.png`.
- Meta titles and descriptions: the copy run's proposed set (deck section 8) replaces the old positioning.
- One H1 per page; headings in order. The drawn screens use plain text for their own headings so they never
  break the page's heading order.
- Skeletons (rule 29): a photo's frame shows a breathing skeleton until the file arrives; a campus clip's frame
  does the same until its still has decoded; `pricing.html?slow=1` shows the plans' skeleton while they load.
  The skeleton no longer uses a shimmer gradient. The orb stays for a late film and a pressed button (the
  founder's loader).
- `screens.js` (the product screens drawn in code) was rebuilt: `calendar` (laptop) and `calendarPhone`
  (an agenda), `classPlan` and `classPlanPhone`, `session` and `sessionPhone` (the weekly live session on a
  video call with David), `daily10`, `challenge`, `test` (a full-length test with the on-screen graphing
  calculator open), `planning` (Score Plan, marked "Coming spring 2027" on the screen), `parent`, `chat`
  (the cohort chat with David answering). Every laptop screen has a phone twin, shown under 761px, so a
  screen is never shrunk to 6px type (rule 44). Laptops sit in a drawn MacBook Pro frame. Every "Daily 10" on
  a screen reads "Your Daily 10" (rule 53). The Wordline screen is gone (Wordline is "coming spring 2027").
