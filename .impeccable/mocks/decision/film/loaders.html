Loaders and messages

Every one is drawn by our own code. Nothing shows for waits under 400 ms. The rules are written at the top of kit.js.

One tap, a short wait

The orb in the button

Appears 400 ms after the press, in place of the arrow. The words stay, so the eye doesn't lose its place.

The film is late

The large orb

Over the film after 400 ms. One line of words joins it after 2.5 s, so a long wait never feels silent.

A page or a big file

The line

Starts at 12%, runs fast, slows but never stops, then finishes in 180 ms. Its ribbing drifts backward, which makes it read faster.

A section's content

The skeleton

Shaped like what's coming, from 300 ms, held at least 500 ms so it never flickers. The sheen sweeps slowly, in reading direction.

Your week

Two live classes, one review, Daily 10 every day.

Something finished

The message

Says what happened. The check draws itself. It stays long enough to read twice, and longer while the pointer is on it.

A field needs fixing

The field message

Next to the field, only after you leave it, and gone the moment it's fixed. It says what happened and what to do.
