Karman logo, round 2

Drawn from scratch in the spirit of Allan Peters: heavy even strokes, one idea folded into the letter, solid colour fields. The lettering in every lockup is custom too, so no part of any option is a typeface. These are first drawings, chosen for the idea; the one you pick gets refined, then built in 3D.

Three Lines

The K drawn as three parallel lines, like lanes on a track: one steady plan, run the same way every week. The stem and the arm never touch, so it stays clean at 16px.

64, 32 and 16px

Breakthrough

The name Karman comes from the Kármán line, where the sky ends and space begins. The K sits in an orbit and its arm breaks out through it.

64, 32 and 16px

The Bookmark

The stem is a bookmark ribbon: the place you saved, and the missed question that comes back on day 2, 7 and 21.

64, 32 and 16px

The Month

A month of days. The filled days draw the K and the ringed one is test day: every program is planned day by day and ends on one date.

64, 32 and 16px

Fixed Point

The K opens toward one star: the goal, the score, the date. Everything in the mark points at it.

64, 32 and 16px

Above the Line

A wordmark drawn from scratch, with no font. One line runs across the name and only the two A's rise through it.

64, 32 and 16px

The Seal

A seal for the things that should feel established: the program certificate, stickers, the parent welcome pack. The Three Lines K sits at its centre.
