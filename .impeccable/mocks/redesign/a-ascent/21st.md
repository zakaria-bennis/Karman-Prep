Searched on the paid tier on 2026-11-01 for every major surface (hero, section layouts, the pricing
presentation, the guarantee block, the campus trio, the close, the header, the footer, the theme
toggle, the FAQ list). Each one below was fetched in full with `get_component`, ported to plain HTML,
CSS and JS, recoloured with Karman's tokens, and had its Lucide, Tabler or Remix icons replaced by
icons drawn for Karman (`I` in `site.js`). The id is credited at the top of the file that carries it.
