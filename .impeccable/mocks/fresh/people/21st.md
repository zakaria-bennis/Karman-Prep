# 21st.dev components used on the "people" page

Each was retrieved on 2026-11-01, ported to plain HTML, CSS and JS, recoloured with this page's
tokens, and stripped of Lucide icons (replaced by icons drawn for Karman).

| 21st.dev id | Name | Where | What was kept, what changed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 26064 | Sticky Content Wrapper (hyperiux) | The split stage at the top of the page | Kept the idea: one column of steps scrolls while the paired picture holds still and swaps. Dropped GSAP and ScrollTrigger for one IntersectionObserver; the swap is a 1.6 s crossfade of two Zoom-style tiles, with a slow 26 s drift on the stills. Phones get the tiles inline above each step. |
| 37545 | Single Open Accordion (ibrandify) | Questions parents ask | Kept single-open behaviour, aria-expanded/aria-controls/region wiring and Arrow/Home/End keys. Dropped the card border, shadow, eyebrow label, category pills, divider lines and the Lucide plus; the toggle is a drawn two-stroke chevron. Closed answers are also visibility-hidden so screen readers and the overlap scan skip them. |
| 37721 | Theme Switch (zweiundeins) | Header (desktop, icons only), menu sheet and footer | Kept the segmented radiogroup with Device / Light / Dark and the sync across every switch on the page. Replaced the cookie with localStorage (`karman-people-theme`), so no cookie is set. Own drawn icons; tone-step background instead of a border. |
