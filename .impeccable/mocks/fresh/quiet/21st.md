# 21st.dev components in the Quiet direction

Searched 21st.dev first (FAQ accordion, theme toggle, plan selector, phone menu). Each port is plain
HTML, CSS and JS, recoloured with the page tokens. No Lucide icons survive: every icon is drawn for
Karman (the mark, the sun and moon, the plus).

| Where | 21st.dev id | Component | What was kept | What changed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Phone menu (`.sheet`) | 27229 | Immersive Full Screen Navigation (hyperiux) | The clip-path wipe from the top, the focus trap that wraps Tab, Escape to close, focus back to the trigger, a plain fade under reduced motion | No GSAP, no images, no socials, no character-split hover. A "Menu" text button instead of a hamburger. Closes on a link tap. Ground and ink from the tokens. |
| FAQ (`#acc`) | 29230 | Ghost Accordion (arihantcodes) | Borderless rows, the plus that turns into a cross, one open at a time | The 01/02 numbering is removed (founder rule). Built on `<details name="faq">`, no Radix. The open row takes one tone step (`--surface`) instead of a border. |
| Theme toggle (`[data-theme-toggle]`) | 37503 | Theme Toggle (luv-jeri) | The idea: sun rays retract and the disc becomes a moon | Redrawn as one 24px SVG with CSS transitions, no Motion library, no view-transition reveal. Remembered in `localStorage` (`karman-theme`). |

Looked at and not used: 37721 Theme Switch (three-way picker, more than the page needs), 69 and
29547 pricing toggles (three tiers, rule 17). The price list follows the taste-skill blueprint
"Plan names, one live figure" instead.
