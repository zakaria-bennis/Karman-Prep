The one open choice: where the tools sit on a phone

Desktop and iPad have room for one bar. On a phone the tools need a row of their own. Both versions below are the real screen.

What changed

  • Top bar: Exit (it was hidden inside More), the title, the clock with Hide, then Calculator, Highlight, Pencil, Reference (math) and More.
  • Highlight is new in the bar: select words, then tap it, as on Bluebook. With nothing selected it says how.
  • Bottom bar: Back and Next carry chevrons. The navigator dots show a tick for right and a cross for wrong, so it is not colour alone, with "3 of 10" beside them.
  • Every button squeezes when pressed and springs back with a small overshoot. Quick repeat taps never queue.
  • Phones no longer scroll sideways: before, the bar's tools stretched the whole screen and cut off the question.
  • The learn area's floating "Today" pill no longer sits on top of the quiz's bar.
  • Challenge questions: today's painting, finished and still, around the question; a thin strip of it under the bar on phone and iPad. The bars take the painting's hue.
