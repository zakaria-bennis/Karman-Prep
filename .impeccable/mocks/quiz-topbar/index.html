The quiz screen's top bar and margins

Three options next to today's version, on the same question. The question, the answer buttons, the pencil, the highlighter and the calculator are the real ones, unchanged. Only the top bar, the bottom bar's colors and the space around the question change. Colors come from today's painting.

Mode
Question

Desktop, 1440 wide

Tablet, 768 wide (math only)

What changes in all three

  • The bar is solid. No glass, nothing moving behind any text (the drifting stars go).
  • Square 4px corners. The clock and counters are in Hanken Grotesk with even-width figures.
  • The small uppercase line above the title goes (no eyebrow labels). Exit sits in the bar instead of inside More.
  • The main button takes the painting's accent with white text and a chevron.
  • Phones: today the bar's tools are wider than a 390 phone when the calculator is on, so the whole question slides off the right edge (see "Today" below). All three options fit. On a phone the section name drops out of the bar; Exit, the clock and the tools stay.

To click through the options live, run node .impeccable/mocks/quiz-topbar/lab/server.mjs from the repo, then open http://127.0.0.1:4324/?option=a (or b, c, today; add &q=rw for a reading passage).
