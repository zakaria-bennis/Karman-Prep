Working with graphs

Enter y=x^2, f(x)=2x+1, x=3, or a point such as (2,3). Constant expressions display a result.

Use powers, parentheses, π, e, sin, cos, tan, asin, acos, atan, sqrt, abs, log (base 10), ln, exp, floor, ceil, round, min and max. Trigonometry follows the selected angle mode.

Enter adds a row. Up/Down moves between rows. Ctrl/Cmd+Z undoes; Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z redoes. Focus the graph to pan with arrows, zoom with +/−, or recenter with Home.

Add an x₁/y₁ table to fit linear, quadratic, exponential, or constant models. Adjust parameter sliders or enter an implicit equation such as x^2+y^2=25. Curves are sampled numerically. Gaps and very narrow features can be missed. Paste plain-text expressions; images and graph files are not accepted.
