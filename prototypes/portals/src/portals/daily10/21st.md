# 21st.dev components in the Daily 10 end screen

The approved end screen (`prototypes/daily10-end/`) credited nineteen ids in
its kit layer plus the Morph Dialog. Each one was re-ported here where it
added something to a real moment, recoloured with the tokens, with the design
system's icons and marks; the rest were left out for a design rule and are
listed with the reason. The design system's own ports (confetti, the sheet,
the glass button, the segmented control) are used as they are.

Drawn for Karman here, no source: the rays behind the prize (a flat tinted
disc and two turning ray wheels, no halo), the voxel burst, the answer path
(one row of ten, or two rows of five joined by a tie on phones), the animated
skill bar (same geometry as the design system's `LevelBar`), the sound icon
(a candidate for the icon set), and the companion engine ported from
`mascot.js`. The review row under the next step is the design system's `Row`.
