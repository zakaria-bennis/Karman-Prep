# 21st.dev components in the school portal

Searched and fetched on the paid tier on 2026-11-01 for the surfaces this
portal adds beyond the design system. Each component is credited in a comment
at the top of the file that ports it; what was left out is said there too.
The design system's own ports (12460 glass button, 23552 segmented, 43112
toggle, 35036 table, 31360 sheet, 35703 toast, 29863 and 35156 skeleton,
20068 count-up, 23549 progress bar, 19749 empty, 18228 locked, 29334 rail)
are used as they are, and the tutor portal's Initials (28355), StudentHeader
and level marks come in read-only through `src/portals/tutor/shared.ts`.
