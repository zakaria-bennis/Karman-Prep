# 21st.dev components in the tutor portal

Searched and fetched on the paid tier on 2026-10-31 for the surfaces this
portal adds beyond the design system. Each component is credited in a comment
at the top of the file that ports it; what was left out is said there too.
The design system's own ports (12460 glass button, 23552 segmented, 43112
toggle, 25353 calendar, 35036 table, 36571 heatmap, 29334 rail, 31360 sheet,
35703 toast, 19201 dropzone, 29526 stepper, 29863 and 35156 skeleton, 20068
count-up, 23549 progress bar, 19749 empty, 18228 locked) are used as they are.
