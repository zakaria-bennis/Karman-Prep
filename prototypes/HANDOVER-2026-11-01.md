  `src/lib/calculator/`, the `/calculator` page; never load Desmos.
- Never write inside the founder's cloud-drive folder under
  `~/Library/CloudStorage/` (it is read-only and shared with his boss).
- Never spend Higgsfield credits. Image generation runs through
  `scripts/generate-assets.mjs` under its $10 budget; every charge goes in
  `assets/spend-log.csv`. Zakaria has approved "all image or short video
  creation needed" for this work, so no need to ask per shot, but log it.
- Never print or commit a secret. (`.env.local` holds keys; never cat it.)
- Never show a real student's data in a prototype; never write or invent a
  testimonial; never claim a feature works without having clicked it.
- Prices, dates and claims come only from `src/lib/marketing/offer.ts`; do not
  edit it without the founder. Values marked `CONFIRM:` are his to confirm.
- Design rules: `docs/design-rules.md` (numbered 1 to 58 plus copy and SEO
  rules) win over everything; `docs/design/site-design-principles.md` says
  how pages move. Key ones this work keeps tripping on: 33 (theme follows the
  device, dark when no preference; AA by calculation in both modes), 34
  (nothing fixed or sticky over text being read), 35 (one highlighted call to
  action on screen at a time), 38 (no visible play or pause button on the
  background film), 44 (screens are readable and show real Karman product
  screens), 45 (questions in pictures are Karman originals, never College
  Board material), 47 (campus captions: the founder's three named students
  with score and college; the clip must match the caption), 28 (no sideways
  scroll). The "remembered footer film control" and "never crop a capture
  through a word" are decisions and judge criteria (section 6), not rule text. No eyebrow labels, no 01/02
  numbered lists, no sliders, no calendar drawing, no filler captions.
- Replace, don't accumulate: delete the old version in the same change.
- The founder leads design: show working screens, options side by side, mark
  your pick, and nothing visual is final until he approves it.
- Commit only by explicit paths with `--no-verify` (the pre-commit hook
  stashes the tree and `git checkout <ref> -- path` stages files, which has
  swept running agents' work into commits before). Commit between workflows,
  not while builders are editing.
